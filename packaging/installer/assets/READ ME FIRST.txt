DIGONTO STUDIO - READ ME FIRST
==============================

Digonto Studio is a family of free programs: pictures, drawings, PDF, documents,
spreadsheets, audio, video and more. They work completely offline: your files never
leave your computer, and the programs never connect to the internet.

HOW TO INSTALL
--------------
1. Double-click the Digonto Studio icon.
2. Tick the programs you want. Digonto Studio is always installed.
3. Click Install. When it says "All done", click OK, then Quit.

macOS
-----
- The first time you open it, macOS may say it cannot verify the program
  (it is not signed by Apple yet). Open System Settings > Privacy & Security,
  scroll down and click "Open Anyway". This is needed only once.
- After installing, eject the "Digonto Studio" disk in Finder.

Windows
-------
- If "Windows protected your PC" appears, click "More info" > "Run anyway".
- Every program gets its own entry in the Start menu, and in Settings > Apps.

Ubuntu / Debian
---------------
- Click "Install Digonto" in your apps or dock to open this setup.
- After installing, every program has its own icon in your apps.

UPDATE, REPAIR, UNINSTALL
-------------------------
Open the setup again (a newer download for updates) and choose Update, Repair or
Uninstall. You can also uninstall one program from its tile in Digonto Studio.
Uninstalling never touches your own files.
